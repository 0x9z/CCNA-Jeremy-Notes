# Day 01 – Network Devices

## What is a Network?

A network is just two or more devices connected together so they can share stuff — files, internet, printers, etc.

Example: Two laptops connected with a cable = a network.

---

## Client vs. Server (The "Ask" and "Give")

| Role | What it does | Real life example |
|------|--------------|-------------------|
| Client | Asks for something | You ordering food at a restaurant |
| Server | Gives/serves something | The kitchen making your food |

> 🍔 Your phone watching YouTube? = Client (asking for the video)
> 📤 Your friend's phone sending you a file via AirDrop? = Server (giving the file)

**Key point:** The same device can be a client sometimes and a server other times.

---

## Switches (The "Splitter")

**What it does:** Lets you plug in many devices together in the same building/office.

**Think of it like:** A power strip, but for internet cables.

**Rules:**
- ✅ Connects many PCs together (24+ ports)
- ✅ Works inside one location (called a LAN)
- ❌ Cannot connect to the internet directly

---

## Routers (The "Bridge")

**What it does:** Connects different networks together (like your home network to the internet).

**Think of it like:** A translator between your house and the outside world.

**Rules:**
- ✅ Connects between networks
- ✅ Gives you internet access
- ❌ Has fewer ports than a switch

---

## Firewalls (The "Bouncer")

**What it does:** Decides what traffic is allowed in and out of your network.

**Think of it like:** A bouncer at a club — checks who gets in and who gets blocked.

**Two types:**

| Type | What it is | Example |
|------|-----------|---------|
| Network Firewall | A physical box | What companies use |
| Host-based Firewall | Software on your PC | Windows Defender |

**Next-Generation Firewall** = A smart bouncer that detects bad guys automatically.

---

## One-Picture Summary


PCs ──→ SWITCH (connects them together) ──→ ROUTER (connects to internet) ──→ FIREWALL (blocks bad guys)
